Privacy Policy

Ball Radar  ·  Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Ball Radar application handles user information. We take your privacy seriously: the game runs on your device, without user accounts, and your location never leaves it.

1. Summary

Ball Radar is free and supported by advertising. A one-time in-app purchase ("No ads") removes advertising permanently. Your GPS position and the balls you place stay on your device. With your consent, the app sends anonymous usage statistics (for example "a game was completed") so we can improve it; see section 5.

  • Your GPS location is never recorded by us or transmitted to any server of ours.
  • No user account is created.
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone or any other device data.

2. Use of GPS

The app requests location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) solely to display your position on the radar in real time and calculate the distance to the game balls. This information is processed exclusively on your device. It is not shared with the advertising SDK nor included in the usage statistics.

3. Locally stored data

Ball Radar stores the coordinates of balls defined by the user, the language preference and whether the "No ads" purchase is active in the device's local storage. This data is never synced to any external service and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have purchased "No ads", the app shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. To do so, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID (AAID).
  • Approximate device information (model, OS version, language, screen size, IP address).
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics.

Ads are requested with a "General audiences" (G) content rating. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any ad is loaded, and you can change your choice at any time from the app menu ("Privacy options"). How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is used and where new players get stuck, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records pseudonymous events such as opening the app, watching the demo, waiting for GPS, starting, completing or resetting a game, sharing or importing a game file and changing the language, together with basic device information (model, OS version, language, country derived from the IP address) and a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase. It does not record your GPS position, the coordinates of your balls, your name or any account, and the advertising ID is not collected for analytics. The statistics are never used for advertising.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, usage statistics are only sent if you accept measurement in the consent form shown at first launch; you can change your choice at any time from the app menu ("Privacy options"). Elsewhere they are sent by default. Google processes this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information; detailed event data is kept for 2 months (Google Analytics default), after which only aggregated reports remain.

6. In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

The "No ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive payment details. The app only stores locally whether the purchase is active and asks Google Play to restore it when reinstalled.

7. Children

Ball Radar is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated in the future, the new version will be published at this same URL with a revised update date.
