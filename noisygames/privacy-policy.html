Privacy Policy

Noisy Games  ·  Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Noisy Games application handles user information. The games run on your device, without user accounts, and the sound picked up by the microphone never leaves it.

1. Summary

Noisy Games is a set of voice party games played by passing the phone around. It is free and has no advertising. Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app sends anonymous usage statistics (for example "a game was completed") so we can improve it; see section 4.

  • Audio from the microphone is never recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • No user account is created.
  • No access to contacts, camera, location or any other device data.

2. Use of the microphone

The app requests the microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) solely to measure how loud you are while you play. While a turn is running, the sound is reduced on your device to a single loudness value every few milliseconds, which moves the on-screen meter and is then discarded. No audio is saved to a file, kept in memory after the turn, or sent anywhere, and the loudness values are not included in the usage statistics except as the final score of a turn.

3. Locally stored data

Noisy Games stores the player names you type, the best score of each game mode and your settings in the device's local storage. This data is never synced to any external service and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which games are played and where players get stuck, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records pseudonymous events such as opening the app, starting a game (mode, number of players and turns), finishing a turn (mode and score) and completing a game, together with basic device information (model, OS version, language, country derived from the IP address) and a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase. It does not record any audio, the player names, your name or any account, and the advertising ID is not collected. The statistics are never used for advertising.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (judged by the country of the mobile network, the SIM card or the device language settings), no usage statistics are sent at all. Elsewhere they are sent by default. Google processes this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information; detailed event data is kept for 2 months (Google Analytics default), after which only aggregated reports remain.

5. Children

Noisy Games is not directed at children under 13. Younger children may join a game on an adult's phone, under that adult's supervision. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated in the future, the new version will be published at this same URL with a revised update date.
