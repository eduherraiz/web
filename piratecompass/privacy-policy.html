Privacy Policy

Brújula Pirata (Pirate Compass)  ·  Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Brújula Pirata application (published in English as Pirate Compass) handles user information. The game runs on your device, without user accounts, and your location never leaves it.

1. Summary

Brújula Pirata is a treasure hunt game: an adult walks a route with the phone to hide a treasure, and then a player follows it with a compass. It is free and has no advertising. Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app sends anonymous usage statistics (for example "a hunt was completed") so we can improve it; see section 5.

  • Your location is used only on the device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No user account is created.
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone or files other than the maps you choose to open.

2. Location

The app requests the precise location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) to record the route while a treasure is being hidden and to guide the player along it. It is used only while one of those screens is open. Recorded routes are saved on the device (see section 4). Location is never included in the usage statistics, never sent to any server and never used for advertising. The app shows hand-drawn maps only and does not use any online map service.

3. Motion sensors

The app reads the compass (magnetometer) and the accelerometer: the accelerometer is used to count the player's steps during the last metres to the treasure and to detect when you shake the phone to dig it up. No special permission is needed for this. Steps are counted on the device, used only to tell the player how many steps remain, and are not stored or sent anywhere.

4. Locally stored data and shared maps

The hidden routes (their name, the pirate's name you type and the coordinates of the stops and the treasure) and your settings are stored in the device's local storage. They are deleted when you delete a map or uninstall the app. A map is only sent to another person when you explicitly share it: the app then creates a .piratemap file containing that route and hands it to the sharing app you pick (for example a messaging app). Anyone who receives that file can see the route, so share maps only with people you trust.

5. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is used, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records pseudonymous events such as opening the app, creating, sharing or importing a map (number of stops and approximate length in metres), and starting or completing a hunt (difficulty, number of doubloons, duration), together with basic device information (model, OS version, language, country derived from the IP address) and a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase. It does not record your location or the routes, the names you type, or any account, and the advertising ID is not collected. The statistics are never used for advertising.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (judged by the country of the mobile network, the SIM card or the device language settings), no usage statistics are sent at all. Elsewhere they are sent by default. Google processes this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information; detailed event data is kept for 2 months (Google Analytics default), after which only aggregated reports remain.

6. Children

Brújula Pirata is not directed at children under 13. Younger children may play a hunt on an adult's phone, under that adult's supervision; please play in safe places away from traffic. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If this policy is updated in the future, the new version will be published at this same URL with a revised update date.
